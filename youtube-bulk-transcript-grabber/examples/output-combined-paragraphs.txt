[Music] Welcome back. Today we are looking at how a song is put together. Most popular songs use three main building blocks. The verse, the chorus and the bridge. The verse moves the story forward, so its lyrics usually change each time. The chorus is the part that repeats, and it carries the main idea of the song.

If someone remembers one line from your song, it is normally from the chorus. The bridge comes later and gives the listener something different. It might change the melody, the chords, or the point of view in the lyrics.

When you analyse a song, start by marking where each section begins. Then compare the words in each verse and notice what changes.

3. Planning a music release: a step-by-step checklist
https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=SmpVid00003
Channel: Sample Music Channel
================================================================

Releasing music takes more planning than most people expect. Here is the checklist I use, starting about eight weeks before release day. Week eight: finish the final mix and master. Week seven: prepare artwork and write the credits correctly. Week six: upload to your distributor and choose the release date.

Weeks five to two: plan short clips and a pre-save link. Release week: check every store page and share the links. After release, look at the numbers but do not panic in the first few days.
